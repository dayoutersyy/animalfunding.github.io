Campus Animal Rescue Story

388 RMB: A Beginning

This page records how a small student team moved from regular shelter volunteer work to a public sharing event, and why 388 RMB became a meaningful starting point.

Story

In 2024, Brandon, then in junior high, came across a post about a stray cat and dog shelter in Chongqing. The shelter had been running for more than forty years, and Grandma Chen had put almost all she had into caring for abandoned animals.

Brandon became a volunteer and kept going once or twice each month, except during the busiest exam period. A normal shift lasted about three hours: cleaning litter, refilling water, and doing basic sanitation for hundreds of cats.

In mid-April this year, he learned that an auntie who took care of cats at the shelter became ill and could no longer continue the work. That moment changed his understanding of responsibility.

Later, Lilith, who had also volunteered since 2024, Thomas, who cared about animals and documented activities through photography, and Dustin, who joined after seeing Brandon's post, became part of the team. What started with one student became a group of four, and later a broader collaboration of around ten students, including poster design and website support.

They documented a real volunteer session without staged shots, designed posters that focused on individual cats and their real backgrounds, and ran a campus sharing event with an opening, documentary screening, story sharing, and a donation explanation.

There was no pressure during the donation moment. Participation was voluntary. Before any formal donation channel was confirmed, students and teachers who wanted to support the action contributed 388 RMB in advance.

The amount was small, but the team treated it seriously. Their plan is to publish follow-up process information through this website and student communication channels with clear records.
